# Periodic Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Periodic Lab is an educational chemistry application designed to work without an account. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

## Data stored on the device

The app stores learning progress, XP, achievements, preferences, favorite reactions, flashcard mastery, and daily activity locally on the user's device. This data is used only to provide the app's learning and progress features. It is not sent to Periodic Lab or third parties.

## Permissions and third parties

Periodic Lab does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising ID, or storage access. It contains no advertising, analytics, account system, or third-party tracking SDK. Internet permission is included by the application framework, but the current app experience does not send user data to a server.

## Children

Periodic Lab does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no account or personal-data collection is present, learning progress remains only on the device.

## Data deletion

Users can clear locally stored app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected by updating the effective date. Before publication, the developer must add a monitored support email here and publish this policy at a stable public HTTPS URL.
